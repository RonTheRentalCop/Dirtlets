I decided that I did not want my robot to be dump as rocks but instead be smart
The gain over the ML method I used was, I was able to run a AI over all camera input from the esp 32 cam via transmitting the data back to the main computer.
The main computer then takes the transmitted data and runs the AI depth image anylisis over it to then have the ML algorithum that I trained to determine what direction the robot should travel in relative to the furthist away point.

What are the Issues with this?

When vibrating or moving too fast it can make the AI misread the depth and give weird information
